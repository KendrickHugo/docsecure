How it works

  1. Confirm your identity and clearance
  2. Select documents and submit payment proof
  3. Wait for OR and receipt review
  4. Generate your encrypted QR after approval
  5. Present it at the registrar counter

A rejection includes a correction notice. After a successful counter scan and document release, status changes to Claimed.
